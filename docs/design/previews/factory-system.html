01 · Principles
Clean records, dirty room

Layout, type, and data stay disciplined; grit lives only in the environment — grain, vignette, hatch, machined edges. If a texture competes with a value, the texture loses.

Machinery is material, not process

Dials, plates, hazard paint, stamps: yes. Gears, conveyors, robots, pipelines: never. Factory is a run substrate, not a factory pipeline.

Amber means "your turn"

sig-warn marks work awaiting a human decision — attention, not failure. The dashboard exists to drain the amber column.

Family-wide hexes: down = blocked/rejected · ok = verified/released/connected · warn = awaiting, attention pending · info = agent working. On the warm ramp, saturation separates signal from environment — no neutral may exceed ink-dim's saturation. The accent stays cool so warm saturation always means a real signal. No fifth signal, ever.

04 · Shopwear — the texture layer
Grain + vignette

Already on this page: feTurbulence film grain at 8% over everything, oil-stained corners at 40%. Fixed, pointer-transparent, off under prefers-contrast: more.

Hatch

45° hairlines at 6.5% ink. Empty states, placeholder zones, archive panels. Never under body text… except in this demo.

Hazard

Forge's escalation grammar: 45° stripes at 8% sig-down, 50%-alpha border. Destructive confirm zones only — never for blocked or awaiting states.

Cards and buttons carry the machined edge — inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.04), a milled top lip catching light. The one depth cue on chrome besides the overlay shadow. Glow exists but is rationed — only indicators emit light: signal dials, the connection dot, and the mark's chip carry drop-shadow(0 0 4px signal @ 55%). Buttons, chips, text, and cards never glow.

One circular grammar — 16 grid, 1.6px ring, drawn inline with a <title> and a mono text twin. The hinge is AWAITING: the core is full (the claim exists) but the ring is dashed (unsealed) — the same dashed-equals-unverified grammar as the Countersign. Verification seals the ring. Task-level progress pies count only verified subtasks; claims don't move the needle.

09 · Controls

Primary = accent fill. Accept/Reject are state actions and wear their signal tints — the only buttons allowed to. Agent verbs (Report…) and human verbs (Accept/Reject/Defer) never share a vocabulary. Min height 44px.

10 · Motion & rules
  • Do — exactly three animations: countersign stamp (120ms), live report flash (sig-info → ink, 400ms), one spinner (800ms). The active dial's wedge does not rotate. Reduced motion: flash becomes a persistent left border.
  • Do — grain 8%, vignette 40% max, hatch 6.5%; glow on indicators only (dials, connection dot, mark chip); all off under prefers-contrast: more. Machined edge is the only card depth cue.
  • Don't — gears, conveyors, robots, pipelines. Machinery is material, not process.
  • Don't — hazard stripes on blocked/awaiting states; hazard is for destructive zones only.
  • Don't — pills, drop shadows, decorative gradients, hover lifts, a fifth signal hue, color without a text twin, texture under body text, or glow on anything that isn't an indicator.
  • Don't — warm saturation in chrome. On the oil ramp, saturation is what separates signal amber from environmental warmth.
